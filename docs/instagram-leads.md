## Suggested next step / 建议后续

Ask whether they are willing to contribute photos of individually identified snakes, with explicit image-use terms, hatch/breeding records or genetic evidence, the visible morph label, and permission to show the photo in a public demo. A public Instagram photo is not an open dataset. Keep any unverified seller descriptions labeled as such; never infer hidden-carrier status from the image.

征集时询问是否愿意提供蛇只 ID、明确图片使用许可、孵化/繁育或遗传证据、可见表型标签，以及是否允许比赛 demo 公开展示。Instagram 公开可见不等于开放数据集。卖家描述须标为“未独立核验”，不能从照片推断隐性携带型。
