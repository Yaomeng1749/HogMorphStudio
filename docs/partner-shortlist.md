MorphMarket says Hognose sale listings should show the actual animal, which makes its unique Animal ID useful for grouping images. The seller's claims still require review, and the site does not grant third-party reuse permission through public listing access. See the [official photo rules](https://support.morphmarket.com/article/52-rules-for-selling) and [seller-owned record export path](https://support.morphmarket.com/article/123-importing-exporting-animals).

To meet the morph-model target, request at least 100 **distinct individuals** with authorized photos and explicit visible-trait evidence, ideally with several examples per base trait and combinations. Multiple angles of one snake improve coverage but count as one independent individual for data splitting.

For the current training gate, the operational minimum is 100 eligible images from at least 30 distinct snakes, with each reported trait meeting explicit positive and negative individual counts in train, validation, and test. A contribution can include multiple images per snake, but all views of one individual must remain in a single split. These are prototype admission thresholds, not a guarantee of adequate statistical power.
