MorphMarket says Hognose sale listings should show the actual animal, which makes its unique Animal ID useful for grouping images. The seller's claims still require review, and the site does not grant third-party reuse permission through public listing access. See the [official photo rules](https://support.morphmarket.com/article/52-rules-for-selling) and [seller-owned record export path](https://support.morphmarket.com/article/123-importing-exporting-animals).

To meet the corpus target, request at least **100 authorized, reviewed photos across at least 39 distinct individuals**. Multiple angles of one snake count as multiple photos but only one independent individual. Each photo needs a traceable source and permission covering its intended use; each trait needs explicit positive and negative labels supported well enough to pass the split gate. This is a minimum admission target, not a guarantee of adequate statistical power.

The trainer has an early broad floor of 100 eligible images and 30 distinct snakes. The actual 70/15/15 class-support gate needs at least **39 individuals** for the partition capacities to hold 5 positive and 5 explicit-negative individuals in train, plus 3 of each in both validation and test. This 39-individual figure is only the mathematical capacity floor: each trait still needs enough separately documented positives and explicit negatives, and all photos of one individual must stay in one split. See the [experiment plan](experiment-plan.md) and [model architecture](model-architecture.md) for the implemented rules.
